Questions, answered

Quick answers before you jump in.

Is eDiary free to use?

Yes — create an account and start organizing notes right away.

Can I access my notes on phone?

The UI is responsive. Sign in from any modern browser on phone or desktop.

What if I forget my password?

Use Forgot password on the Sign In page. We’ll email a reset link to your registered address.

Can I remove my data later?

Open Profile → Delete account. Confirm with DELETE and your password to wipe your diary data.
